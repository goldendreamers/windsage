Windsage Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026 · Contact: shakedwald@gmail.com

In plain words

Not legally binding. The official policy is the legal text below. If they differ, the legal text wins.

Windsage watches the wind spots you follow and can ping your phone when your rules hit. That is the whole product.

We keep only what we need to do that:

  • Your username, and your Google email if you sign in with Google.
  • The stations or map pins you follow, plus the alert rules you set.
  • A device id so settings can sync, and a push subscription so alerts can reach your phone.
  • Ordinary server logs (when a request happened, and errors).

We do not sell your data. We do not put ads or ad trackers in the app. We do not need to follow you around town — a station id or a pin you chose on the map is enough.

Your Windsage data lives on the server behind https://windsage.nimrod.bio/. Live wind numbers come from Windguru.

You can use the app as a guest with no account. You can unfollow spots, pause alerts, or wipe local app data. To delete an account, email shakedwald@gmail.com. You can also turn off phone notifications in your phone’s settings.

Windsage is not for children under 13.

Official policy

Windsage (“the app”) helps you follow Windguru spots and live stations and receive alerts when your wind rules are met. This policy explains what we store and why.

What we collect

  • Account details you choose to provide (username, optional email from Google sign-in).
  • Your followed stations, alert rules, and device identifiers used to sync settings.
  • Push notification subscription details so we can send wind alerts to your phone.
  • Basic technical logs needed to run the service (for example request times and errors).

What we do not collect

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use advertising trackers in the app.
  • We do not need your precise GPS location to operate (Windguru spot/station IDs are enough).

Where data is stored

App data is stored on the Windsage cloud host used for https://windsage.nimrod.bio/. Live weather readings come from Windguru’s public interfaces.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we send alerts when your rules are met (and optional test alerts you request). You can turn notifications off in your phone settings at any time.

Your choices

  • You can use guest mode without creating an account.
  • You can unfollow stations, pause monitoring, or delete local app data.
  • To request account deletion or data removal, email shakedwald@gmail.com.

Children

Windsage is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
