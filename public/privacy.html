Windsage Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026 · Contact: shakedwald@gmail.com

Windsage (“the app”) helps you follow wind stations and receive alerts when your rules are met. Weather is fetched by the Windsage cloud so the app on your phone does not talk to Windguru, NOAA, or map search providers. This policy explains what we store and why. We collect as little as we can to run alerts.

What we collect

  • Account details you choose to provide (username, optional email from Google sign-in). Guest mode stores only a random device id on this server.
  • The stations you follow and your alert rules, so the cloud can poll and notify you. Map pins you save stay on your account and are not put in a public directory.
  • The latest reading for each of your follows (not a history archive), so Home can show the current wind.
  • Push notification subscription details so we can send wind alerts to your phone.
  • Optional good/meh on an alert (kept briefly on your account, without nicknames).
  • If you opt in to Discord alerts, the Discord user id you link so we can DM you when a station alerts.
  • Basic technical logs needed to run the service (for example request times and errors).

What we do not collect

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use advertising trackers in the app.
  • We do not copy your follow list, nicknames, or map pins into a public catalog.
  • We do not need your GPS to operate. A location follow is only stored if you drop a pin yourself.
  • We do not keep search queries or IP addresses on disk (rate limits stay in memory).

Where data is stored

App data is stored on the Windsage cloud host used for https://windsage.nimrod.bio/. Live weather readings come from Windguru’s public interfaces.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we send alerts when your rules are met. You can turn notifications off in your phone settings at any time. If you link Discord alerts, we also send a Discord DM to the account you linked. You can unlink from Account or with /unlink in the Windsage Discord server. If you turn on “Wake me up when there is wind” for a station (advanced mode, under Monitoring), we keep repeating phone notifications or Discord DMs until you tap Stop ringing, for up to 30 minutes.

Your choices

  • You can use guest mode without creating an account.
  • You can unfollow stations, pause monitoring, or delete local app data.
  • You can stop Discord alert DMs from Account or with /unlink in Discord.
  • You can stop a wake-up ring from the in-app Stop ringing button or the notification action.
  • To request account deletion or data removal, email shakedwald@gmail.com.

Children

Windsage is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
