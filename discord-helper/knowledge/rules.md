Hello everyone, and welcome to Windsage! This server is dedicated primarily to following the wind with friends — spots, holds, and the phone ping when it is actually up. That’s https://windsage.nimrod.bio/. Please enjoy your stay, and give a careful reading to the rules below before you engage.

First and foremost, In this server, I want you to remember: we are all suffering. Some of us were born to cruelty, some of us were born to kindness, and some of us were born to one disguising as the other. We may have been hurt by our family, by our so-called friends, by our relationships or our neighbors or by strangers who never knew our names. We have been taught to lash out in defense of ourselves, we have been taught to shout our beliefs furiously from the rooftops and rally to battle against any who disagree with them, or perhaps we have been taught to stay silent and say nothing at all. And there is a time and a place for all of these things.

This world, however, is packed with us. We are everywhere. And every single one of us has our own story, our own thoughts, our own way of thinking. Being hurt makes it hard to empathize with people unlike ourselves, and we are all hurting. But I want us to get better at it, and I hope that you want this, too.

**1. Assume people mean well.**

Do not forget that most users mean well. This doesn't mean that people won't hurt you. This absolutely does not mean you should forgive every injustice. But it's not always intentional,

Communicate, clarify, confirm. If someone says something that worries you, or disturbs you, or hurts you, or angers you, tell them so, clearly and concisely. Give them an opportunity to clarify their meaning: nine times out of ten, it's just going to be a miscommunication. But if you confirm what you believe to be ill intent, you should not stand for it.

**2. Education is the key to kindness.**

We cannot be good to one another if we do not know how, and it is important to always remember that if someone is hurting you, they very likely do not realize it. A small wind group pulls in people who live on different coasts, who learned different slang for the same gust, who do not feel like they belong in a “scene,”. If possible, I want everyone to try and explain why a situation is making them uncomfortable, and I definitely expect everyone to listen if someone does explain this. Of course, you are never under any obligation to talk to someone that is hurting you. We all have our own limits, and it is okay to be at yours.

You can always ask for a conversation to end. If someone is ever making you uncomfortable, even if they do not mean to, you may ask them to stop. This should immediately cause a polite and immediate cessation of the conversation. If they refuse, or you're the person having the conversation and you feel that they are requesting the end of the conversation in bad faith, this is a great time to contact a moderator.

**3. Ping a moderator.**

Moderators are always available to help. Please, please, please do not hesitate to ping a mod or me (windsage) if a situation is making you uncomfortable. Your feelings are valid, and we are here to listen. Someone is usually around, and you genuinely shouldn't feel bad about getting our attention.

**4. This is a safe-for-work server.**

Sexually explicit discussions, images, and memes are not allowed anywhere on the server. There is a blurry line here: people talk about bodies, kit, and getting out on the water. Discussion in the abstract is okay. However, never forget that underage users may be on the server, and your interactions with everyone here should be considered in that light.

**5. This is a safe place for trauma.**

People here have real lives and real history. There are many people who have serious trauma. As such, mature and serious topics are to be treated seriously at all times, and memes/jokes in bad faith about suicide, death, assault, abuse, or similar issues will absolutely never be tolerated.

**6. Bigotry will not be tolerated.**

This server is a safe space for unique individuals, including LGBT+ users, autistic users, plural users, and other neurodivergent users. Harmful actions and statements against these groups and those like them will be swiftly corrected. Actively malicious actions against these groups will see you swiftly banned.

**7. Basic internet etiquette.**

Don't spam channels. Don't post exact private-spot access, gate codes, or “secret” coordinates in public channels if the person who shared them asked you not to. Don't break the Discord TOS. And if a moderator asks you to stop doing something, just stop. Don't argue, or you can expect a swift mute or ban. The mod team and I are not an algorithm that will only punish you for breaking the letter of the law; attempting to skirt the rules or ignoring requests to stop bad behavior will get you banned. If you're ever unsure about anything, please feel free to ask.

**7.5 Politics stay in #politics.**

Certain topics are quarantined because they are things some people wish to avoid while still being sufficiently important or of interest, so we do not want to ban them outright. These topics should only be discussed in their appropriate quarantine channels.

Politics are quarantined to #politics. Don't do it in other channels. All the other rules (especially 4, 5, and 6) still apply there.

**8. Be kind to each other.**

Above all, this is the most important rule. Thank you for helping make this server a safe and enriching place for yourself and others.
